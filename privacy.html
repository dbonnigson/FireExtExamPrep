Privacy Policy

Fire Extinguisher Exam Prep · Last updated November 11, 2026

Fire Extinguisher Exam Prep collects no personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your answers, scores, completed lessons and settings are stored only on your iPhone. They never leave the device and are deleted when you delete the app or use Settings → Reset all progress.

If you turn on the daily study reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to a server.
